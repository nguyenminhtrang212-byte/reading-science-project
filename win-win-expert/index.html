🏅 Be a Win-Win Expert

You are an expert. Two people have a problem. Help them find a win-win solution (giải pháp đôi bên cùng có lợi).

1 Understand2 List options3 Check: fair? feasible?4 Give your solution5 Checklist

Choose a case chọn tình huống

Step 1 · Understand the problem hiểu vấn đề

Step 2 · Check the options kiểm tra lựa chọn

For each option choose: Is it fair? Is it feasible? (fair = công bằng · feasible = khả thi) · A win-win option is fair and feasible.

Step 3 · Useful phrases cụm hữu ích

🔊 nghe · “Xem nghĩa” · “+ Add” thêm câu vào bài viết của em ở Step 4.

Step 4 · You are the expert! Write your solution viết giải pháp

Talk to the two people. Use 4 or more phrases from Step 3.

Mẫu / Sample: “Can we stop for a second? What do you need? … How about …? This works for me because … It’s fair because … It’s feasible because … It’s a win-win solution.”

Step 5 · Expert checklist tự kiểm tra

🏅 Win-Win Expert! Great job!

Bonus · Role-play in 3 nói theo nhóm 3

A = person 1 · B = person 2 · C = Expert
C (Expert): Can we stop for a second? What do you need, A? And you, B?
A and B: I need … because …
C: Let’s list some options. How about …? Is it fair? Is it feasible?
A and B: This works for me because … / Hmm, not really.
C: Great! It’s a win-win solution.
