Mom’s Phone

My name is Lan. Last night I had a small argument with my mom. It made me feel quite upset, and it was all because of my phone.

I said, “Mom, I need my phone to study with my group. I have a test tomorrow.”

Mom said, “No. Go study. Your phone distracts you.”

I said, “But my friends are texting in the group! I need to answer.”

Mom said, “I said no. Give me your phone.”

I got angry. I said, “You don’t understand! You’re always like this!” Then I gave her my phone and went to my room.

Mom didn’t say anything. She just sighed.
